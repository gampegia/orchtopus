Orchtopus · mockups

Each page shows the same four screens in one theme and mode. The layout and components are shared; only design tokens and theme effects change. This is how themes should work in the app (concept E27, F55, F56). The first two themes are the preferred directions; Stellwerk is the calm default.

Island Landscape
Requested direction. Floating, rounded panels over an illustrated landscape; day palette in light, night palette in dark. Content panels stay opaque.		darklight
Tinted Glass
Requested direction. Frosted panels over a vivid violet, blue and rose wallpaper. Terminal and decision cards stay nearly opaque.		darklight
Stellwerk (default)
Calm default from the design-unslop pass: cool graphite, colour only where you decide, gates as a signal track.		darklight
Signal Bloom
Optional theme. The work area is lit in the colour of what waits for you.		darklight
HUD Brackets
Optional theme. Measuring grid, cut corners, segmented progress, cyan chrome accent.		darklight
